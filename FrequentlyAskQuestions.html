Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our products, orders and services.


1. What products does The Vinyl Stoep sell? We specialize in new and pre-owned vinyl records, CDs, turntables and selected music accessories. Our collection includes South African classics, international favourites, jazz, soul, R&B, hip-hop and other genres.
2. Do you sell new and pre-owned records? Yes. We offer both new and carefully selected pre-owned records. Every pre-owned record is inspected and graded according to its condition before being placed on sale.
3. How much do your vinyl records cost? Our prices vary depending on the artist, album, rarity and condition of the record. Each product listing displays its current price and condition.
4. Can I sell or trade my old records? Yes. We welcome customers who would like to sell or trade selected vinyl records. Records are assessed based on their condition, rarity, demand and overall value.
5. Do you offer delivery? Yes. We offer delivery options for customers who are unable to visit our store. Delivery fees and estimated delivery times depend on the customer's location and the size of the order.
6. What payment methods do you accept? We accept several convenient payment methods, including card payments, and electronic payments.
7. Can I request a specific record? Absolutely. If you are looking for a particular album or artist that is not currently listed in our collection, you can contact us and submit a request. We will do our best to help you find it.
8. Do you sell turntables? Yes. We stock selected turntables and other equipment for customers who want to start or improve their vinyl collection.
9. Can I visit the store and browse the collection? Yes. Customers are welcome to visit The Vinyl Stoep during our operating hours and browse our available collection.
10. What condition are your pre-owned records in? Pre-owned records are inspected and graded before being sold. We provide condition information with each product so customers know what to expect before purchasing.
11. Do you accept returns or exchanges? Returns and exchanges may be accepted in accordance with our store policy. Customers should contact us as soon as possible if they receive a damaged or incorrect product.
12. How can I contact The Vinyl Stoep? You can contact us by phone, email or through the contact form on our Contact Us page. You can also follow us on social media for updates about new arrivals and special offers.
Still Have Questions? Can't find the answer you're looking for?
We're happy to help. Get in touch with The Vinyl Stoep and our team will assist you with your question.
